Frequently asked questions

How accurate is the plant identification?

Verdant uses PlantNet's identification engine, trained on millions of verified plant photos, and typically returns species matches with confidence scores so you can judge reliability yourself.

What's included in a free scan?

Free scans identify the species and give a confidence score. Full issue diagnosis, treatment plans, and prevention tips require a Basic or Pro plan.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. Subscriptions are billed monthly with no lock-in — you can cancel anytime from your account settings and you'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

Does the AI chat assistant work for any plant?

Yes, the Pro plan's chat assistant is available for every plant in your collection, and it has context on that specific plant's species and scan history.

Is my data private?

Your plants, scans, and account details are private to your account. We don't sell your data to third parties.

What payment methods do you accept?

We accept UPI, all major debit/credit cards, and net banking through Razorpay, India's leading payment gateway.

Do you offer plans for schools or organizations?

Yes — reach out through our contact page for classroom gardens, NGO food-security projects, or corporate wellness programs.
