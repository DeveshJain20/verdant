// OUR STORY

We got tired of guessing too.

Verdant started the same way most plant problems do — with a dying fiddle leaf fig and a dozen contradictory blog posts about why. We built the tool we wished existed: something that actually looks at your plant and tells you what's wrong, instead of asking you to diagnose it yourself from a stock photo.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Specific beats generic

"Water weekly" isn't advice, it's a guess. Every recommendation Verdant gives is tied to the actual species and condition in front of it.

HOW WE WORK

AI trained on real plant pathology

Our diagnosis engine combines species identification with known disease and deficiency patterns, not just plant identification alone.

Built for growers, not showrooms.

Whether it's one windowsill herb or a balcony full of pots, Verdant scales to what you're actually growing.
